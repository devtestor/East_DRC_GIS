# Domain Glossary

- Administrative unit: Configurable jurisdiction or locality such as province, city, territory, commune, sector, chiefdom, groupement, quartier, locality, avenue, or village.
- Application: A request for service submitted by a citizen, professional, institution, or officer.
- Audit event: Append-only record of actor, action, target, evidence, source, time, and classification.
- Cadastral approval: Human authorization to publish geometry after validation and review.
- Current geometry: The single approved geometry version currently in force for a parcel.
- Maker-checker: Separation between the person who prepares or requests a change and the person authorized to approve it.
- Parcel: Land unit tracked by immutable internal UUID.
- Public projection: Data-minimized parcel view safe for public search and maps.
- Restriction: Legal or administrative constraint limiting transactions on a parcel. In the current implementation it is a protected operational control/evidence record, not a court decision generated by the platform.
- Right or interest: Ownership, lease, mortgage, easement, claim, caution, or other legally relevant relationship to land.
- UPI: Proposed unique parcel identifier. It is configurable, versioned, and not assumed to be an official national standard.
